The Lima Lounge

About Us

We Are Basically Your New Favourite Place

The Lima Lounge is a pop restaurant with entirely too much personality and absolutely no apologies about it. We serve good food, curate ridiculous amounts of fun, and host events that make your average Tuesday feel like a headline act. You're welcome.

Our Origin Story (No Cape Required)

Once upon a time, someone looked at a regular restaurant and thought — "this needs more vibes." And thus, The Lima Lounge was born. We didn't set out to change the dining scene. We just did it anyway. Since day one, we've been turning ordinary evenings into stories worth telling at brunch the next morning.

Events? Oh, We Do Events.

From themed pop-up dinners to live music nights that somehow get out of hand in the best possible way — if it's happening at The Lima Lounge, it's worth showing up for. We host events the way other people breathe: frequently, naturally, and with great enthusiasm. Check our events page before your social calendar fills itself with things you'll regret.

The Food (Since You Asked)

Yes, the food is excellent. No, we will not be modest about it. Our kitchen takes serious inspiration from bold, global flavours and absolutely zero inspiration from the phrase "it's fine." Every plate that leaves our kitchen has been thought about, argued over, and perfected. Your taste buds will notice. They always do.

Our One Rule

Have a good time. That's it. That's the whole rule. We'll handle the ambiance, the food, the events, and the playlist. You just show up, sit down, and let The Lima Lounge do what it does best. (Hint: it's a lot.)
